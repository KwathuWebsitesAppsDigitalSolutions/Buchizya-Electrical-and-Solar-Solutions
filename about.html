About Us

Buchizya Electrical and Solar Services founded in 2011 & formally registered in 2019 under the Malawi Registration of Companies Act, is a trusted provider of sustainable energy and infrastructure solutions.


We consistently delivered services before and after registration, building a strong reputation for reliability and innovation. Our mission is to make clean, renewable energy accessible and affordable for everyone.


We specialize in solar energy systems, electrical engineering, borehole drilling locally made incubators, and bio-digester septic systems.
